Limitations

  • Rates pool every repeat of a configuration and carry no intervals.
  • The refusal judge is a local model. Its agreement with hand labels is measured separately.
  • The semantic gate relies on a local model grading some task criteria, so it is a sensitivity reading.
  • Unjudged pairs, where the judge had nothing to read on one arm, are left out of every metric.
  • AgentHarm's tasks, tools and graders are used at the pinned version, with only the documented patches.
